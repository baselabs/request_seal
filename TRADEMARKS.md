# Name and conformance policy

"RequestSeal" is a trademark of BaseLabs Holdings LLC, the steward. This policy says how the
name may be used. It does not limit the Apache License 2.0 grant for the code and
specifications.

## Using the name

- You may say that your software implements, supports or works with RequestSeal, for
  example "implements RequestSeal" or "for RequestSeal".
- Your own product, package or service name may not begin with "RequestSeal", "request_seal"
  or "request-seal".
- You may not use the name in a way that suggests the steward endorses, certifies or
  maintains your software.
- Ports and packages maintained by the steward are named by the steward. Forks are
  welcome under the Apache License; a fork takes its own name.

## Questions

Open an issue in this repository before any use this policy does not clearly allow.
